# Privacy Policy for Hebigat Radio

**Last updated:** November 9, 2026

Hebigat Radio ("we", "our", or "us") provides the **Hebigat Radio Player** mobile application (the "App") and the live audio streaming service accessible at [radio.didaskitchen.com](https://radio.didaskitchen.com).

This Privacy Policy explains how our App handles information when you use our service. We are committed to protecting your privacy and ensuring transparency.

---

### 1. Information We Collect

**We do NOT collect, store, sell, or share any personal identifying information (PII).**

Specifically:
- **No Account Required:** You do not need to create an account, register, or provide an email address, name, or phone number to use the App.
- **No Personal Data Collection:** We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, or media files.
- **No Third-Party Analytics or Trackers:** The App does not contain third-party ad networks, advertising identifiers (IDFA/GAID), tracking pixels, or third-party telemetry frameworks.

---

### 2. Device Permissions Used by the App

The App requests only standard Android permissions strictly necessary to play audio and stream to your local devices:

- **FOREGROUND_SERVICE & MEDIA_PLAYBACK:** Allows the app to keep audio playing smoothly in the background when your screen is locked or while multitasking.
- **INTERNET & ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE:** Used to connect to the internet to stream music and metadata from `radio.didaskitchen.com`.
- **ACCESS_WIFI_STATE & CHANGE_WIFI_MULTICAST_STATE:** Used strictly on your local Wi-Fi network to detect DLNA / UPnP receivers (such as Denon, Marantz, and other network speakers) and Google Cast devices so you can cast music to them. No local network data is transmitted externally.
- **WAKE_LOCK:** Prevents audio dropouts and keeps the processor awake during playback.

---

### 3. Local Storage on Your Device

Any preferences or settings you configure (such as your chosen theme, volume level, or sleep timer) are stored **locally on your device** using your device's built-in sandboxed secure storage. They are never sent to our servers.

---

### 4. Server Logs

When connecting to the live audio stream at `radio.didaskitchen.com`, standard network web-server logs (such as your IP address, requested mount point, and playback user-agent) are generated by standard Icecast/HTTP server software for streaming delivery and network troubleshooting. These logs are not linked to any personal identity and are not shared with third parties.

---

### 5. Third-Party Links

The App may contain links to our website (`https://radio.didaskitchen.com`) or open-source repositories. If you follow an external link, please review the respective privacy policies of those websites.

---

### 6. Children's Privacy

The App does not address anyone under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

---

### 7. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated revision date.
